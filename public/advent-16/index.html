The story

A chime, and a note under the door

You are the new developer in the Christkind's workshop. The Christkind runs it all, and nobody has ever seen the boss.

Tickets arrive as a little chime and a short note under the door, and they are always vaguer than the Christkind thinks.

You have 24 days before the bell rings on the evening of the 24th.

The workshop

Your coworkers

  • The Wichtel

    The intern, which is your agent. Quick, keen, says “done!” a bit too early.

    done!

  • Nikolaus

    The kind reviewer. Comes on 6 December with his golden book, where he writes down what was done well.

  • Knecht Ruprecht

    The release gate. Nothing passes him without a check. Gruff, fair, and secretly proud of you.

  • The Gremlin

    Not a person: the vague ticket, the disabled test, the rule nobody enforces. It gets weaker as you get better.

Listen for the bell

On the evening of the 24th, a little bell rings. Until then: one door a day.

About these traditions

This calendar is inspired by German winter traditions. In Bavaria and Franconia the Christkind brings the presents but is never seen; usually only a little bell is heard. On 6 December Nikolaus visits, often with his gruff helper Knecht Ruprecht. Here they are characters in a workplace story. You don't need to know or share any tradition to open a door.

About this calendar

Every door is a tiny project you fetch with one command and run with your coding agent. Claude Code is the reference; GitHub Copilot CLI and Codex work too.
